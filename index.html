Advocating for Change: Rescue Me Conference

We are proud to highlight that our CEO, Mr. John Haggee Ouma, recently served as a distinguished panelist at the "Rescue Me" Conference.

Hosted by the Dear Me Foundation in collaboration with ID Yangu Kenya, the event gathered leaders to discuss critical issues surrounding community healing and mental wellness. Mr. Ouma shared valuable insights on the power of collective support and the role of non-profits in fostering resilient communities. His participation underscores Hearth and Heal's commitment to being at the forefront of these vital conversations.
